Our mission

Many young developers in Cameroon cannot get a first job without experience, and cannot gain experience without a first job. Access to opportunities and knowledge is often limited to a few people.

We address this by building working software publicly, sharing what we learn, and teaching from the simplest concepts. Our public code is our portfolio.

About us

UNITED KODERS was founded by Desmel Anodem. The team grew from two friends to four, and in July 2025 competed at the Westech Hackathon in Bafoussam, where we presented Agrilink and placed 5th of 8 teams.

Today we are 10 members working remotely from Douala, Yaoundé and Bafoussam.
